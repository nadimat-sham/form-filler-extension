# Form Filler

A free Chrome extension that fills job application forms with your saved information in one click. It works on common job sites and on Bangladesh government job forms (Teletalk).

Your information is stored only on your own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

---

## Install

### 1. Download

On this GitHub page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.

### 2. Unzip

Unzip the downloaded file and move the folder somewhere you'll keep it, such as **Documents**.

> Don't delete or move this folder later. Chrome runs the extension from it.

### 3. Add it to Chrome

1. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (top-right).
3. Click **Load unpacked**.
4. Open folders until you see **`manifest.json`** directly inside, then select that folder.

> Unzipping often creates a folder inside a folder. Choose the inner one, the folder that directly contains `manifest.json`.

### 4. Pin it

Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar, then the **pin** next to **Form Filler**.

---

## Use

1. Click the **F** icon, then **Edit my information**. Fill in your details, add your resume, and click **Save**. You don't need to fill in everything: skip whatever you like and click **Save**.
2. Open a job application and click **F**, then **Fill this page**.
3. Fill in any fields it missed. When the box **"Do you want to update your saved info?"** appears, click **Save** so those fields fill automatically next time.
4. Check the form before submitting.

---

## Update

Download the new ZIP, replace the files in your Form Filler folder, then click **↻** on the Form Filler card in `chrome://extensions`. Your saved information is kept.

## Troubleshooting

**"Manifest file is missing or unreadable"**
You selected the wrong folder. Click **Load unpacked** again and open folders until you see `manifest.json` directly inside, then select that folder.

**Popup says "No information saved yet"**
Each computer starts empty. Click **Edit my information**, fill in your details and click **Save**.

**"Didn't find any fields I could fill"**
Reload the job page (F5) and try again. Pages opened before installing or updating the extension can't be filled until you reload them. Some forms only show their fields after you click "Apply".

**Developer mode is greyed out, or the extension disappears after restarting Chrome**
Your Chrome is managed by a school or office, which blocks extensions like this. Use a personal Chrome profile: click your profile picture (top-right), then **Add**, and continue without signing in to the school or work account.

**"Disable developer mode extensions?" message**
Click **Keep**, or close the message. Chrome shows it for any extension that isn't from the Web Store.

**"Chrome doesn't allow extensions on this page"**
Chrome blocks extensions on its own pages and on the Chrome Web Store. Open the actual job application page.

**A dropdown wasn't filled**
Your saved answer doesn't match the form's options. Choose it yourself, then click **Save** in the update box. In your information, write dropdown answers the way forms show them, for example `Dhaka`, `S.S.C`, `GPA(out of 5)` or a university's full name.

**Other browsers**
It also works in Edge (`edge://extensions`) and Brave (`brave://extensions`). It doesn't work in Firefox or Safari.

**Errors or Warnings button on the extension card**
Click it and include a screenshot when you report the problem in [Issues](../../issues).
